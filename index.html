Manage your land and property details

Use this free service to view and manage the official digital record of your property, replacing paper-based Land Registry applications.

You can now do all of the following digitally and free of charge:

  • View your definitive digital property title instantly.
  • Alter your tenancy type (for example, sever a joint tenancy) and manage equity shares.
  • Track real-time privacy access logs and grant data permissions to third parties.
  • Pre-authorise digital property inheritance protocols.

Pre-authorise digital property inheritance

This advanced planning screen lets you specify what happens to your property registry entry upon your death, cutting out months of probate delays.

Your instructions are stored securely in HM Land Registry's encrypted digital vault and can only be activated upon verified confirmation of death from the General Register Office (GRO).

Important: This protocol acts as a digital directive to HM Land Registry. It can be modified or revoked by you at any time using this secure portal. It does not replace a formal will.
